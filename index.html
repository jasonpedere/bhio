FAQ

Got questions? We've got answers.

Everything you need to know about Bhio and our free public launch.

Is Bhio really 100% free?

Yes, absolutely! Claiming your handle, using all 5 curated templates, and adding unlimited links is completely free. There are no trial periods and no credit card required.

How many links can I add?

Right now, you get unlimited links and social icons. Add your Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Spotify, WhatsApp, portfolio, shop links, and more with zero restrictions.

What happens when you add paid templates later?

All core features, existing templates, and created links will always remain free for our creators. Any future paid offerings will be purely optional premium designer templates for creators who want extra bespoke styles.

How long does it take to set up?

Less than a minute! Pick your custom username, add your links, pick your favorite aesthetic, and your page is immediately live at bhio.link/yourname.
